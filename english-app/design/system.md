# Design system: מחברת אנגלית

Decisions so far, with the reason for each. Change an entry only when a new decision replaces it.

## Wardrobe (ארון הבגדים), decided 25.11 (version 3b)
- **Room:** the attic picture, darkened toward the edges like night (a vignette); the character and the wardrobe stay lit. *Why:* the background was too busy; the dark edges calm it without redrawing it.
- **Categories:** parchment tags hanging on strings from brass nails down the open right door, top to bottom, swinging on hover; the chosen tag is wine (#8C3550) with cream text. *Why:* they look like part of the drawing, not round app buttons.
- **Frame (26.11, option C of six):** square corners; light wood outside (angled gradient #B07A45 → #4E2F1B), a thin gold line (#E6B53A), darker wood inside, ink outline. Used on the door tags, the word plaque on the rug and the buy note. *Why:* reads like an old picture frame and matches the wardrobe's brass.
- **Spoken word:** shown on a framed plaque on the rug under her feet, not floating white text.
- **Clothes:** dresses, tops, skirts and wings hang on a rail on hangers; shoes, tights, hats and held things stand on the shelf. Each has a parchment price tag hanging on it. Names in Amatic SC, cream on the dark wood. *Why:* it reads like a real wardrobe and a shop.
- **Buying:** always in the same place, a framed parchment note on the bottom shelf: "רוצה את זה?" + English name + price + one "לקנות" button; a ✕ in the corner for "not now". *Why:* one fixed place, one short question for a reader who isn't fluent yet.
- **Money:** worms (the girls chose). In the wallet and the buy note, a drawn pile of worms on a mound that wriggles; on price tags a single worm.
- **Surprises in the room:** cat, candles, mirror, chest, lantern, moon. Each says its English word and does something small; candles and lantern flicker. A rare twinkle hints that something can be tapped. No flying bat or spider (they didn't look good).
- **Motion:** breathing every 6 s, one personality move every 7-13 s and on tap, a hop of joy with sparkles on buying. Off switch and reduced-motion respected.
- **Type:** Karantina for Hebrew titles, Amatic SC for handwritten labels, Andika for English words that are read aloud, Rubik for body text.
- **Palette:** ink #2B1D2E, parchment #F4E7C8, wine #8C3550, gold #E6B53A.
