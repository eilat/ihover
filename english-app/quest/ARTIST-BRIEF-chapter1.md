# Midnight Misfits: Chapter 1, "The Crescent Moon": art list

A new game with the same four girls. A giant crow snatches the neighbours' little boy. The sisters set out across the Midnight World, where it is always midnight and only the moon changes. On the way they gather ingredients for potions that help them follow the crow. Chapter 1 has three places and ends with the first potion, which shows the way to Chapter 2.

Same style as the wardrobe art: hand-drawn, a little grotesque and funny, warm night colours (plum, violet, pumpkin orange, moon yellow).

## Rules for every picture
- **Backgrounds:** 1672 × 941 px, the same size and viewpoint as the attic. Keep them **calmer than the attic**: fewer small objects, a clear open floor on the left third where a character stands (feet about 85% down the picture), and 4–6 clear, separate objects that can be tapped. No text in the pictures.
- **Objects and characters:** transparent PNG. Objects 512 × 512, centred, the whole object inside the square with a little margin. Characters: tell me their size relative to the girls (the girls are the 1000 × 2000 kits).
- One file per object; file names in English as listed below.

## 1. Story picture (opening)
- `story_crow-snatch.png` (1672 × 941): night, the neighbours' house and garden, a giant crow flying off with the little boy in its claws; the boy reaching out. Scary enough to be exciting, not frightening for an 8-year-old.

## 2. Three places (backgrounds)
1. `bg_attic-cauldron.png`: the same attic, with a big **cauldron** on the rug area moved to the right, a shelf of empty bottles and a book of recipes. (Or a version of the existing attic with the wardrobe closed, if that's easier.)
2. `bg_pumpkin-patch.png`: the garden behind the house: pumpkins, a crooked fence, a scarecrow, a broken swing, a few black feathers on the ground, the crescent moon above.
3. `bg_mushroom-woods.png`: the edge of the woods: giant glowing mushrooms, an old well, fireflies, a frog on a stone, a hollow tree.

## 3. Ingredients (objects, 512 × 512)
- `ing_crow-feather.png`: a big black feather with a purple shine
- `ing_pumpkin-seeds.png`: a handful of glowing pumpkin seeds
- `ing_moon-mushroom.png`: a small pale mushroom with a crescent mark
- `ing_firefly-jar.png`: a jar with three fireflies
- `ing_well-water.png`: a small bottle of silver water from the well
- `ing_frog-song.png`: a little green frog holding a musical note (silly is good)

## 4. Potion and cauldron
- `cauldron_empty.png`, `cauldron_bubble_1.png`, `cauldron_bubble_2.png`: the same cauldron, empty and with two bubbling frames (same size and position, so they can flicker).

## 5. Characters

## 6. Map and moon
- `map_chapter1.png` (1672 × 941): a hand-drawn map with the three places joined by a dotted path, and the path continuing into the dark (Chapter 2). The three places can be small drawings on the map.
- `moon_1.png` … `moon_8.png`: the eight moon phases, new moon to full moon, transparent, 256 × 256. Chapter 1 uses the crescent.
