# Directions menu

Pick five rows that differ from each other. Mix and match within a row only if it still reads as
one clear idea. Adapt every choice to the subject's own world: its materials, tools, places and
vocabulary are where distinctive choices come from.

| # | Direction | Layout skeleton | Type | Color world | Density |
|---|-----------|-----------------|------|-------------|---------|
| 1 | Product hero | One big product image or demo, text beside it | Clean sans, large weight contrast | Light, one brand color | Airy |
| 2 | Editorial | Long scroll with chapters, big pull quotes | Characterful display + quiet body | Colors taken from the subject itself, not cream paper | Medium |
| 3 | Poster | One screen, one message, oversized type | Heavy condensed display | Two bold colors, high contrast | Minimal |
| 4 | Catalogue | Grid of cards, filters, quick scanning | Compact sans, tabular numbers | Neutral with a functional accent | Dense |
| 5 | Story | Steps or a timeline the visitor scrolls through | Friendly rounded sans | Warm, photographic | Medium |
| 6 | Tool first | The interactive thing (calculator, booking, configurator) is the page | Utility sans + mono for data | Cool, calm | Medium |
| 7 | Playful | Asymmetry, stickers, hand-drawn marks | Expressive display, mixed sizes | Saturated, unexpected pairing | Medium |
| 8 | Luxury quiet | Wide margins, slow rhythm, few elements | Refined serif or thin sans | One deep tone that is not black, with a metallic accent | Sparse |
| 9 | Dashboard | Panels, numbers, status at a glance | Mono or grotesk, tight | Dark or light, data colors only | Dense |
| 10 | Local and human | Faces, place, handwritten notes, testimonials up front | Warm serif + handwritten accent | Natural colors of the place | Medium |

## The signature element

Every version gets one thing a visitor will remember, and only one. Examples: a live price that
updates as you type, a menu that unfolds like a paper map, a hero photo that tracks the cursor,
a giant number that counts up once. Keep everything around it calm.
